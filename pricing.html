FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services and pricing.

What is the minimum booking period?

Essential plan requires a minimum of 2 days. Professional and Executive plans can be booked for a single day.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. Our pricing is transparent. The quoted price includes all personnel, equipment, and coordination. Travel and accommodation may be additional for locations outside Lagos.

Can I customise a plan?

Yes. All plans are fully customisable. Contact us to discuss your specific security needs and we'll create a tailored solution.

How do I book?

Simply click the "Book" button on your chosen plan, fill in your details, and our team will get back to you within 2 hours to confirm and finalise the booking.

What if my event runs overtime?

Overtime is charged at an hourly rate. We'll agree on the rate before your event starts so there are no surprises.

Do you offer long-term contracts?

Yes. We offer discounted rates for long-term or recurring security contracts. Contact us for a tailored proposal.
